Crystal
Carvalho

Founder & Producer

Crystal brings a decade of experience across advertising, production and branded content — from developing the right creative approach to budgeting, negotiations, shoot management and post.

She began her career in 2015 at Leo Burnett, spending three years building her foundation in advertising and production, then moved to McCann as a Senior Producer for three years — evaluating scripts, collaborating with directors and production houses, negotiating budgets and managing projects from pre-production through delivery.

She later expanded her experience through Native Films and Skylark Productions, working across the production-house ecosystem and different creative teams. In August 2025, she brought it all together to establish Brain & Bot Films.
