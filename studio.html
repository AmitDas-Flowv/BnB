The Studio

A young production house, backed by years of experience.

Brain & Bot Films is a production house established in August 2025 by producer Crystal Carvalho, bringing together a decade of experience across advertising, production and branded content.

We work closely with agencies, brands, directors and creative teams to take an idea from script to screen — approaching every project with equal attention to the big picture and the smallest detail, bringing together the right creative talent, production design, crew and resources to make the idea work both creatively and practically.

Agile, collaborative and deeply involved in the process.
